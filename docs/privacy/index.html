Privacy Policy

Our Privacy Policy, and it's terms.

This project stores the following personal data

  • Username
  • Password hash (bcrypt, never stored in plain text)
  • Your projects and components tied to your account

Purpose

We use this data only to provide account access and project/component functionality.

Data Retention

Your data is kept until you choose to delete your account. Once deleted, all associated data is permanently removed.

Your Rights

  • You can request a copy of all your personal data via GET /users/me/export.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data via DELETE /users/me.

Third Parties

We use GitHub Pages to host the frontend and Cloudflare D1 to store the database. They act as processors; we remain responsible for compliance.

Contacts

For questions regarding your data, please reach out via DM-ing `_justParrot` on discord.
